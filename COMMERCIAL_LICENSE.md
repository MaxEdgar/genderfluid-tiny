# Commercial Licensing

genderfluid-tiny is available under two licensing options:

## Free: Polyform Noncommercial License

Free for personal, educational, hobby, and noncommercial use. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for full terms.

You do NOT need a commercial license if you are:

- An individual using it for personal projects
- A student or educator using it for learning
- A researcher at a noncommercial organization
- A hobbyist or open-source contributor
- A nonprofit or government institution

## Commercial License

A commercial license is required if you use genderfluid-tiny in:

- A commercial product or service
- A company or business (regardless of size)
- Any use that generates revenue
- Internal tools at a for-profit organization
- Redistribution as part of a commercial offering
- SaaS or API services

### How to get a commercial license

Contact the maintainer to discuss commercial licensing terms:

- GitHub: https://github.com/MaxEdgar/genderfluid-tiny/issues (open a private discussion)
- Email: maxedgar008@gmail.com

### What a commercial license includes

- Right to use genderfluid-tiny in commercial products and services
- Right to redistribute as part of commercial offerings
- Priority support
- Custom model training options (available on request)

## Why dual licensing?

The Polyform Noncommercial License lets individuals, students, researchers, and hobbyists use genderfluid-tiny freely. Commercial users contribute to the project's continued development through licensing fees.

This model ensures the project stays free for those who need it most, while sustainable for the maintainer.
